# Research Roadmap: Applications of Large Language Models in Healthcare

---

## 1. Research Objective

**To systematically investigate and evaluate the current and potential applications of Large Language Models (LLMs) in healthcare, focusing on their efficacy, limitations, and ethical implications. Aim to propose actionable recommendations for future integration and improvement.**

---

## 2. Literature Review Tasks

- **Survey foundational papers:**  
  Review seminal works on LLMs (e.g., GPT-3, BERT, PaLM, Llama) and their general capabilities.

- **Identify healthcare-specific applications:**  
  Examine peer-reviewed articles, preprints, and technical reports where LLMs have been applied to healthcare, including:
  - Clinical documentation assistance
  - Medical question-answering
  - Decision support systems
  - Patient education and communication
  - Automated triage
  - Drug discovery and research summarization

- **Evaluate comparative technologies:**  
  Study older NLP technologies in healthcare to contextualize the impact of LLMs.

- **Assess ethical, legal, and regulatory discussions:**  
  Explore literature on privacy, bias, explainability, and regulatory compliance in LLMs within healthcare.

- **Compile datasets and benchmarks:**  
  Catalog datasets commonly used for training/evaluation (e.g., MIMIC-III, PubMedQA, MedQA).

---

## 3. Research Gap Tasks

- **Identify application gaps:**  
  Find healthcare domains where LLMs are underutilized or not yet studied (e.g., rural medicine, less-commonly-spoken languages).

- **Performance limitations:**  
  Document areas where reported LLM performance is insufficient for clinical use; highlight unmet needs.

- **Bias and fairness:**  
  Investigate existing gaps in understanding and mitigating bias in LLM-generated medical recommendations.

- **Validation and real-world deployment:**  
  Examine shortcomings in real-world evaluation, clinical trials, and patient outcomes.

- **Ethical/Legal/guidance gaps:**  
  Spot missing frameworks for ethical usage and regulatory oversight of LLMs in healthcare.

---

## 4. Experiment Planning

- **Formulate research questions:**  
  Example: "Can LLMs outperform traditional NLP systems in medical question-answering for non-specialist audiences?"

- **Design benchmarks and evaluation metrics:**  
  - Choose relevant datasets for medical QA, text summarization, patient communication, etc.
  - Establish metrics: accuracy, F1, AUROC, BLEU, user satisfaction, clinical safety.

- **Prototype and integrate LLMs:**  
  - Select appropriate LLMs (open-source, proprietary) for testing.
  - Implement them in targeted healthcare tasks (e.g., generating discharge summaries, answering patient FAQs).

- **Conduct comparative experiments:**  
  - Compare LLMs with traditional systems (rule-based, classical ML, smaller models).
  - Include human-in-the-loop evaluation (clinician and patient feedback).

- **Run ablation studies:**  
  - Examine the impact of model size, training data, fine-tuning methods, and prompt engineering.

- **Analyze ethical aspects:**  
  - Test for bias, hallucinations, privacy breaches.
  - Assess explainability.

---

## 5. Deliverables

- **Comprehensive literature review manuscript**  
  (journal-ready, summarizing state-of-the-art with critical analysis).

- **Research gap report**  
  (with actionable, prioritized gap list).

- **Experimental results paper**  
  (with clear methodology, statistical analysis, and discussion for each healthcare application tested).

- **Evaluation toolkit**  
  (benchmarks, scripts/datasets, guides for LLM evaluation in healthcare).

- **Guidelines and recommendations**  
  (for responsible, clinically-safe, and ethical integration of LLMs in healthcare).

- **Presentation/deck for stakeholders**  
  (clinicians, hospital administrators, policymakers).

---

**Optional Extension:**  
Collaborate with healthcare partners for pilot deployment; propose funding applications.
